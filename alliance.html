Alliance

oneworld + FedEx, One Airline

World Express Group's identity is built on a single idea: what if a major passenger alliance carrier and the world's biggest express cargo operator were the same airline?

The Concept

Two icons of aviation, fused into one.

World Express Group is a fictional virtual airline concept that combines the passenger-alliance model of oneworld with the express-logistics model of FedEx. In the real world, these are two entirely separate kinds of operators: oneworld is a passenger airline alliance connecting full-service carriers like Qantas, British Airways, and American Airlines onto a shared network, while FedEx is a dedicated cargo airline running its own fleet of freighters on a hub-and-spoke express delivery model. World Express Group imagines a single carrier that does both — flying scheduled passenger routes with alliance-style global reach, while also operating a dedicated freighter division built around overnight express delivery.

The oneworld Half

Global Passenger Alliance DNA

Our passenger operation borrows directly from how oneworld connects its member airlines into one seamless network for travelers.

  • Shared route network philosophy across hub airports
  • Reciprocal-style lounge and priority boarding concepts
  • Through check-in and baggage handling on connections
  • A widebody and narrowbody fleet built for global reach
The FedEx Half

Express Cargo DNA

Our cargo operation is modeled on the express logistics network FedEx pioneered — speed, reliability, and a dedicated freighter fleet.

  • Dedicated freighter aircraft (777F, MD-11F, A300F)
  • Overnight express & next-flight-out scheduling
  • Sydney sort hub feeding a global route network
  • Simulated time-definite delivery windows
Why This Matters For Pilots

One roster, two very different flying experiences.

Because World Express Group runs both models under one virtual airline, our pilots get to experience two distinct styles of operation without needing to join two separate virtual airlines.

Passenger Ops

Scheduled daytime flying, full cabin crew roleplay, and alliance-style route planning across the network.

Cargo Ops

Night freight flying, tight turnaround windows, and dedicated freighter handling procedures.

One Roster

Pilots can qualify on both divisions and switch between passenger and cargo flying freely.
