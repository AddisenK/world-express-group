Alliance

oneworld + FedEx, one airline concept.

World Express Group imagines what a global passenger alliance carrier and an express cargo operator could look like under one virtual airline brand.

The Concept

Two aviation models, brought together.

World Express Group is a fictional virtual airline concept combining the passenger-network philosophy of oneworld with the speed and reliability of express cargo logistics. The result is one community where pilots can experience both scheduled passenger operations and dedicated freight flying.

Passenger Network

oneworld-Inspired Flying

Our passenger operation takes inspiration from global alliance connectivity and hub-based airline planning.

  • Global route-network philosophy
  • Passenger operations through major hubs
  • Widebody and narrowbody aircraft options
  • VATSIM scheduled and community flights
Express Cargo

FedEx-Style Freight

Our cargo network is built around the express freight approach: dedicated freighters, time-critical planning and overnight flying.

  • Dedicated cargo-capable aircraft types
  • Overnight and next-flight-out operations
  • Frankfurt hub and global feeder planning
  • Freighter dispatch and cargo roleplay
