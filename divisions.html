Our Divisions

World Express Group operates two divisions under one virtual airline — a passenger network and an express cargo network.

Passenger Division

World Express Airlines

Full-service scheduled passenger operations connecting Sydney to a global network, styled after the oneworld alliance's seamless connectivity model.

  • Widebody long-haul & regional narrowbody fleet
  • oneworld-style codeshare route network
  • Priority lounge & hub access roleplay
  • Seamless interline-style booking flow
Express Cargo Division

World Express Freight

Dedicated freighter operations delivering time-critical virtual cargo overnight, modeled on the express logistics network built by FedEx.

  • Dedicated widebody freighters (777F, MD-11F, A300F)
  • Overnight express & next-flight-out scheduling
  • Sydney sort hub with global feeder routes
  • Simulated real-time shipment tracking
