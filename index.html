Two Networks, One Airline

Passenger travel and express cargo, under one tail.

World Express Group operates as a dual-purpose virtual carrier: a full-service passenger airline built around the oneworld concept and a dedicated express freighter division inspired by global overnight logistics.

Passenger Division

World Express Airlines

Full-service scheduled passenger operations connecting Frankfurt to a global network.

  • Widebody long-haul and regional narrowbody fleet
  • oneworld-inspired codeshare route network
  • Hub-and-spoke passenger operations
  • VATSIM group flights and live ATC operations

World Express Freight
