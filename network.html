Flying on VATSIM

World Express Group is built around the VATSIM network, where every scheduled passenger and freight flight is flown alongside live air traffic control.

What is VATSIM?

A global live air traffic simulation network.

VATSIM connects flight simulator pilots and volunteer air traffic controllers in real time. Pilots file flight plans, communicate through voice ATC, and operate with procedures that mirror real-world aviation from clearance delivery to arrival.

Realism

Live ATC on Every Flight

Unlike offline simulator flying, VATSIM places real people in the loop throughout the flight.

  • Live controller coverage across global FIRs
  • Real-world SIDs, STARs and approach procedures
  • Voice communication and standard phraseology
  • Scheduled community events and group flights
World Express Network

Passenger and Freight Routes

Our network supports both scheduled passenger services and dedicated express cargo flights.
