Flying on VATSIM

World Express Group is a virtual airline built entirely around the VATSIM network — the world's largest online air traffic control and pilot simulation community. Every scheduled flight, every dispatch, and every ATC interaction happens on VATSIM.

What is VATSIM?

A real-time global ATC simulation network.

VATSIM (Virtual Air Traffic Simulation Network) connects thousands of flight simulator pilots with volunteer air traffic controllers in real time, 24 hours a day. Pilots file real flight plans, communicate over live voice ATC, and fly procedures that mirror real-world aviation — from clearance delivery through to landing. World Express Group operates its entire passenger and cargo network on this platform, giving every pilot on our roster an authentic dispatch-to-touchdown experience.

Why VATSIM

Realism Built Into Every Flight

Unlike offline flying, VATSIM puts a real human controller in the loop for every departure, en-route clearance, and approach.

  • Live voice ATC across most major FIRs worldwide
  • Real-world SIDs, STARs, and separation standards
  • Coordinated pilot & controller events
  • Pilot ratings from P0 (new pilot) through P4
Our Network Division

World Express VATSIM Division

Our internal VATSIM division coordinates group flights, hub events, and training for both the passenger and cargo sides of the airline.

  • Weekly group flights on Sydney (YSSY) departures
  • Coordinated cargo "night freight" events
  • Dedicated Discord channels for ATC bookings
  • Mentorship from senior pilots for newer members
Pilot Requirements

What it takes to fly for World Express Group.

Because our entire operation runs on live ATC, we require pilots to already be comfortable operating on the network before joining. This keeps flights safe, realistic, and enjoyable for controllers and fellow pilots alike.

500+ hrs

Minimum logged VATSIM flight hours required to apply, verified against your VATSIM ID.

Valid VATSIM ID

An active, verified VATSIM account in good standing is mandatory for all applicants.

P1 Rating+

Applicants should hold at least a P1 pilot rating, demonstrating competence with radio phraseology and procedures.
