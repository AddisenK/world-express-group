Newsroom

The latest announcements, milestones, and updates from World Express Group.

20/08/2026

World Express Group is officially founded

World Express Group launches as a Sydney-based VATSIM virtual airline, combining a oneworld-style passenger network with a FedEx-style express cargo division. The airline debuts with an 11-aircraft fleet spanning narrowbody, widebody, and freighter types.

20/08/2026

Applications now open via NewSky

Pilots holding 500+ hours of logged VATSIM flight time and a valid VATSIM ID can now apply to join the World Express Group roster through our NewSky application page.

20/08/2026

Discord community server goes live

The official World Express Group Discord is now open, giving pilots, dispatchers, and crew a home base for group flights and coordination.

20/08/2026

Fleet revealed: 11 aircraft types confirmed

The full fleet has been announced, spanning the 737, 737 MAX, A320, A321, A330, A340, A380, 747, 777, MD-11, and A300.

20/08/2026

Sydney (YSSY) confirmed as home hub

World Express Group will operate its primary passenger and cargo hub out of Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport.

20/08/2026

Alliance concept published

World Express Group publishes its founding concept: a passenger network inspired by oneworld, fused with an express cargo network inspired by FedEx.
